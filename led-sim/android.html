LED Audio for Android

Sends whatever your phone is playing to the LED designer, so the preview reacts to live music, the same way led-audio.exe does on a PC.

Download LED Audio (APK, 24 KB)

Android 10 or newer. iPhone can't do this: iOS doesn't let apps capture other apps' sound.

Install

  1. Tap the download button, then open the file.
  2. Android will ask to allow installs from your browser ("Install unknown apps"). Allow it, then tap Install. If Play Protect warns that it doesn't recognise the app, choose Install anyway: it isn't on the Play Store.

Use

  1. Open LED Audio and tap Start. Allow the microphone permission and the screen-sharing prompt. That's how Android grants access to other apps' sound; the mic isn't used and no video is recorded.
  2. Play music in a music app. The bars in LED Audio should move. If nothing plays yet, Play a test beat checks capture works.
  3. Open the designer in Chrome. If Chrome asks to let the page access apps and services on this device, allow it. "What the lights hear" should say Live: your phone's sound.

If it doesn't react

Some apps block other apps from capturing their sound, and Chrome is one of them: music playing in a browser tab won't show up. Use an app instead: YouTube and YouTube Music allow capture. If another app stays flat, it blocks capture too.

If the designer says the browser is blocking the app, tap the icon left of the address bar, open the site's permissions, and allow access to apps and services on this device (local network access). Then reload.

The app only listens on this phone (127.0.0.1), so nothing else on your network can connect to it. Tap Stop or the notification when you're done.
