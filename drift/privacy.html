Drift: privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Drift is a free music visualizer for Android. It draws moving pictures that react to sound. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: nothing leaves your phone.

What Drift does not do

  • No ads, no accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No internet access: the app doesn't ask for Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No sharing or selling of data to anyone.

Sound

Drift needs sound to react to. You choose the source:

  • Microphone. Drift listens through the microphone. This uses Android's microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO), which Android asks you to allow.
  • Phone audio. Drift listens to what other apps on your phone are playing. Android only allows this for apps that hold the microphone permission, and it asks for your consent each time through its screen-sharing prompt. Drift uses that consent for sound only: it does not record, store or use your screen.
  • Demo beat. A built-in, silent rhythm. It uses no permissions.

Whichever source you choose, the sound is analysed in memory, on your device, as it plays (for example, how loud it is and where the beats fall). It is never recorded, saved to storage or sent anywhere. When you stop listening or close the app, it is gone.

Notifications

While Drift listens to phone audio, Android requires it to show a notification ("Drift is visualizing this phone's sound"). That is the only thing the notification permission is used for.

What is stored on your phone

Your settings and the looks you choose to keep are saved in the app's private storage on your device. No other app can read them, they are not backed up or sent anywhere by Drift, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Children

Drift is not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
